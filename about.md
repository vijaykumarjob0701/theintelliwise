I am Vijay Kumar, a full-stack engineer in Dublin moving deeper into applied ML. I write about the parts of PEFT, agents, and RAG that show up when you try to ship them: data, evaluation, latency, cost, and the glue between a notebook and a system that other people can run.

These notes are for other engineers. I will prefer a concrete setup, a failed experiment, or a measurement over a survey of papers. If a piece is still a draft, it is not on this site.

The public repo only ever holds approved posts. Rough notes stay private until they are ready to put a name on.
